Where I started

I grew up in Denver and I'm now finishing my Information Science degree at CU Boulder, with a minor in Business Analytics. Alongside school I've spent years in customer-facing and operations roles, first on the sales line at Waterway Car Wash and now running food expo at Boulder Country Club.

Those jobs taught me how a business actually runs: where the delays are, what customers care about, and how small fixes add up on a busy day.

Why data

I've always liked working with numbers and spotting patterns. What hooked me on analytics is the moment a trend shows up in the data and you can explain why it's happening. I like using statistics to understand change, then presenting it so an organization can make a better decision.

Find the trend. Explain the why. Help people decide.

What I've built

My coursework projects range from analyzing NHL statistics in Python to designing relational databases in MySQL and PostgreSQL, building interactive dashboards in Power BI and Tableau, modeling breast cancer biopsy data with hypothesis tests and regression, and mapping campus accessibility with Python heat maps. Several were team projects, including a full-stack app built by five of us with 125+ commits.
